**Original publication:** [Springer / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-02725-2_30) · [DBLP record](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/iwann/JambhrunkarSSK25)

*Detection, Attribution, Narration: An End-to-End Pipeline for Explainable Money Mule Identification* cites and substantively discusses **MuleTrack** in its related work. The paper identifies MuleTrack as a **Markov-chain-based framework for detecting money mule accounts in India’s UPI ecosystem**, placing it alongside subsequent approaches to operational money-mule detection and explainability in banking.

**Evidence:** [KDD 2026 MLF paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17586)

**Research-impact significance:** This is a direct, topic-specific citation in later money-mule research, showing that MuleTrack is being recognised within subsequent work on financial-crime detection.

## Comparative Analysis of Transformer-Based Pre-Trained NLP Models

**Original publication:** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.26438/ijcse/v8i11.4044)

### Independent citation — Springer Nature financial-NLP research

A 2025 **Springer Nature** study on sentiment classification of UK Central Bank Digital Currency discourse explicitly discusses the work in its related-work section, stating that **Singla and Ramachandra** conducted a comparative analysis of transformer-based pretrained NLP models and highlighted the strengths of the models in different contexts.

**Evidence:** [Comparative analysis of transformer models for sentiment classification of UK CBDC discourse on X — Springer Nature](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44257-025-00035-4)

### Independent citation — 2026 scalable NLP research from Morocco

A 2026 study by researchers affiliated with universities in Morocco cites the paper as **Singla, S. & Ramachandra, N.** and discusses its comparison of **BERT, RoBERTa and ALBERT** for multiclass sentiment analysis. The later study benchmarks CNN and transformer architectures using **Apache Spark NLP** for distributed social-media sentiment analysis.

**Evidence:** [Sentiment analysis of trending tweets using Spark NLP and deep learning: a benchmark study of CNN vs transformer models](https://nti.khai.edu/ojs/index.php/reks/article/view/reks.2026.1.10) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.32620/reks.2026.1.10)

**Research-impact significance:** These citations show the paper being referenced across distinct later applications of transformer-based sentiment analysis, including **financial NLP** and **scalable distributed NLP**.
