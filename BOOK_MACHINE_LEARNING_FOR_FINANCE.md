# Machine Learning for Finance

## Book by Saurav Singla

[![Google Books](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google%20Books-View%20Book-4285F4?logo=google&logoColor=white)](https://books.google.com/books/about/Machine_Learning_for_Finance.html?id=7xISEAAAQBAJ)

**Machine Learning for Finance: Beginner's Guide to Explore Machine Learning in Banking and Finance** introduces core machine-learning concepts through practical applications in banking, financial services and business analytics.

The book is designed to help readers understand how data-driven methods can be applied to financial use cases, while building a foundation in statistical learning, predictive modelling and applied analytics.

## Book at a Glance

- **Author:** Saurav Singla
- **Publisher:** BPB Publications
- **Publication year:** 2021
- **Primary domain:** Machine learning in banking and finance
- **Level:** Beginner to intermediate
- **Format:** Practical, application-oriented introduction

## Topics Covered

The book introduces readers to areas including:

- foundations of machine learning
- data preparation and exploratory analysis
- supervised and unsupervised learning
- regression and classification
- model evaluation and interpretation
- financial and banking use cases
- practical analytics workflows

## Intended Audience

The book is relevant for:

- students beginning their journey in machine learning
- banking and finance professionals exploring applied AI
- analysts transitioning into data science
- managers seeking an accessible introduction to predictive modelling
- practitioners interested in financial analytics applications

## Author Perspective

The book reflects Saurav Singla’s broader work at the intersection of machine learning, finance, analytics and production AI. It forms part of his contribution to making applied data science more accessible to learners and working professionals.
