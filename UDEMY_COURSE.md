# Data Analysis for Business and Finance

## Udemy Course by Saurav Singla

[![Udemy Course](https://img.shields.io/badge/Udemy-View%20Course-A435F0?logo=udemy&logoColor=white)](https://www.udemy.com/course/data-analysis-for-business-and-finance/)

**Data Analysis for Business and Finance** is a foundational analytics course designed to help learners understand how statistical and analytical methods can support business and financial decision-making.

The course has reached **21,000+ learners** and develops an intuitive understanding of data analysis, from descriptive statistics and probability through regression and time-series forecasting.

## Learning Outcomes

Learners are introduced to the core concepts and methods required to interpret data and make evidence-based decisions, including:

- Descriptive and inferential statistics
- Exploratory data analysis using graphical and numerical techniques
- Measures of central tendency, dispersion, asymmetry and variability
- Covariance, correlation and relationships between variables
- Probability, conditional probability and probability distributions
- Sampling, estimation and the Central Limit Theorem
- Hypothesis testing and confidence intervals
- Regression analysis and statistical inference
- Time-series analysis, trend modelling, smoothing, seasonality and decomposition
- Application of analytical methods to business and finance problems

## Intended Audience

The course is designed for:

- Business and financial analysts
- Aspiring data analysts and data scientists
- Business-intelligence professionals
- Managers and executives seeking stronger data-driven decision-making skills
- Learners building a practical foundation in statistics, regression and forecasting

## Educational Impact

This course reflects my commitment to making data analysis understandable and applicable beyond specialist technical audiences. Its emphasis is not only on formulas and techniques, but also on developing intuition around why analytical methods are used and how their outputs should be interpreted in practical business settings.

The course complements my broader work as an AI and Data Science leader, author, researcher and technical educator.
